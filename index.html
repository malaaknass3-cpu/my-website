About Us

At Take a Slice we make pizza with love. We want everyone enjoy good pizza anytime. We use fresh ingredients and make our dough by hand every day. Our sauces are rich and full of flavor and we try our best to make every slice tasty and satisfying. Take a Slice is not only for eating, it is a place to feel happy, share with friends and family and come again for your favorite pizza because "One slice is never enough".
